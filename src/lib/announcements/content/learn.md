If you don't know how to play Go, I've hand-written content for a [learning](/learn) page. I wrote this for friends and family who want to play, but hopefully it's useful!

There are sections on the rules, some fundamentals of tactics, and some notes on etiquette.

Your progress on this page is stored entirely on your device, which means you can use it without an account. But that also means I can't learn about mistakes or problems in the lesson unless people tell me. If you try the lessons please let me know about what could be better!
