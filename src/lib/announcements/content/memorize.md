A common technique in studying Go is to memorize games from players stronger than you. This gives you an opportunity to put yourself in their shoes and think through why they made the decisions they did. To support this, every game in **Analysis** mode has a **Memorize** button.

There are a few neat things about this feature. You can replay games from any point in the game and get hints according to what you need at each move. In addition to replaying the full game as both sides, we also support replaying moves just as one of the players.

Since it's built into Analysis mode, every game in the [Library](/library) supports this as do all games anyone has played or uploaded through this site.
