One of the things I'd really like out of this site is to have a nice place for humans to review games with other humans. When a game is in _Analysis Mode_, both players can freely put down new stones and markers and see what the other player is doing.

But usually in games there are some critical points that are worth revisiting and getting to quickly. When you press the **Bookmark** button on a game, it creates a record of that point. With bookmarks:

- 1. A link to that bookmark is automatically created. If you click [here](https://libaduk.com/play/e5dua3?analysis=1&bookmark=0be30a00-365d-47b8-b9ea-c46058c862e8) you can see a question I have from a pro game.
- 2. There is a button on the page that you can click, or you can navigate between bookmarks with Shift + arrow key. This makes it easier to find the places you care most about in a game.
